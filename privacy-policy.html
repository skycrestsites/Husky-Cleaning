Privacy Policy

Husky Cleaning and Property Care (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains how we handle information you share when you request a quote or book a clean.

Information we collect. When you book online, we receive the details you submit, typically your name, email, phone number, service type, address, and any notes about your home or business.

How we use it. We use that information only to respond to your request, schedule service, and follow up about your cleaning. We do not sell your information.

Sharing. We may share details with trusted cleaning professionals assigned to your job, or when required by law.

Contact. Questions about this policy can be sent through the booking form on our homepage.
